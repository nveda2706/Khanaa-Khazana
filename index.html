Merchant Onboarding & Compliance Documentation

Please read our legal operations frameworks, billing timelines, and privacy policies closely.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

21/09/2026

Cancellation Protocol

You may cancel an order before the canteen starts preparing it. Once preparation has started, the order cannot be cancelled. Contact us immediately if you need help.

Refund Authorization Conditions

  • The order is cancelled by you before preparation starts.
  • The order is cancelled by the canteen (e.g. item unavailable).
  • You were charged but the order was not created (failed or duplicate payment).

Exceptions & Non-Refundable Items

Food that has been prepared and is ready for pickup, or orders not collected by the customer, are not eligible for a refund. If there is a genuine quality issue, contact us and we will review it case by case.

Refund Method & Timeline

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method via Razorpay within 5–7 working days.

Shipping & Pickup Policy

21/09/2026

Digital Delivery & On-Premises Pickup

We do not ship any physical goods. All orders are prepared fresh and collected in person at the physical hub: 666, Upper Indiranagar, Bibwewadi, Pune, Maharashtra- 411037.

Preparation & Operational Timeframe

Preparation time depends on the items ordered and current demand. You will receive an instantaneous push notification when your order status changes. Digital order processing occurs between 0 to 24 Hours from order receipt validation.

Collection Requirements

Please present your unique transaction token interface or alphanumeric voucher inside the mobile application layer when collecting it from the counter lines.

Privacy Policy

21/09/2026

Information We Collect

Your name, email address and phone number (if provided), your order history, and device notification tokens used to send order status updates.

How We Use It

To create and manage your account, process and fulfil orders, send order status notifications, and provide support.

Payment Processing Telemetry

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Your card, UPI or net-banking details are entered on Razorpay's secure checkout and are never stored by us. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to that data.

Data Storage & Sharing

Your data is stored securely with Google Firebase. We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run the app (Firebase, Razorpay) or when required by law.
